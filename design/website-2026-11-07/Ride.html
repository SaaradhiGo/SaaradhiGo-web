How a ride works

You agree the fare before the trip starts. That is the fare you pay.

  1. 1Set pickup and destinationType both or use your location. We check the area first.
  2. 2Compare every classAuto, Mini, Sedan, SUV, each with its own price.
  3. 3Book, and the fare locksYou get a fare ticket. It will not go up.
  4. 4Meet your Saaradhi, share your ride codeName, car and number plate shown before they arrive.
  5. 5Pay what the ticket saidNo extra charges added at the end.

What "locked" means
  • The quote you book is held for 5 minutes. Book within that and you pay exactly it.
  • Move the pickup or wait too long, and we show the new fare before you confirm.
  • If the trip itself changes (a new drop, tolls), the receipt lists each change and why.
