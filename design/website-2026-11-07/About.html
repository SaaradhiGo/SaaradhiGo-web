ABOUT SAARADHIGO

Saaradhi means the charioteer: the one who drives you, steadily and honestly.

We started SaaradhiGo because a ride should not end in an argument about the price, and a driver should not work without knowing their pay. So we show every fare before you choose, lock the one you book, and show drivers what they will earn before they accept.

The fare, agreed first

Rider and driver see the same price before the trip, and settle on that number.

Fair to drivers

Earnings and commission are shown on every request, so no one works blind.

Built for people, everywhere

We start in India and grow city by city, keeping the same fair rules wherever we go.

Fair rides for riders and drivers. The fare is agreed before the trip starts.
