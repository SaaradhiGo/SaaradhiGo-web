Safety on every ride

Four things are built into every SaaradhiGo trip, from the moment you book to the moment you arrive.

Your code starts the ride Share your ride code with the driver in person. It is checked on our servers, so the trip cannot start without you.
Every trip is live Your driver's position and route show on your map for the whole journey. Share it with family in one tap.
Hold for SOS
SOS reaches a person Press and hold SOS during a ride. Our operations team gets an alert with your trip, driver and live location.
Documents checked
Every driver is checked Our team reviews each driver's licence, vehicle registration and insurance before their first trip.
In an emergency, call your local emergency number firstThen press SOS in the app, so our team can see your trip and help you and the emergency services. Report a safety issue
Fair rides for riders and drivers. The fare is agreed before the trip starts.
